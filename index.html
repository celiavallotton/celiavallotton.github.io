01 / ABOUT

Curiosity, made tangible.

A practical approach
to engineering.

I’m a second-year mechanical engineering student at Santa Rosa Junior College, completing coursework with plans to transfer to a four-year university. I’m drawn to mechanical design, manufacturing, product development, renewable energy, and applied research.

I like connecting mathematics and science with making: modeling a part, thinking through how it will be produced, then learning from the physical result. Work in engineering clubs, fabrication, woodworking, ceramics, and collaborative projects has shaped how I approach real-world problems.

“

Engineering philosophy

Good design accounts for how a system will be made, used, and tested. Iteration is part of the work: CAD helps clarify a physical problem, while collaboration and hands-on feedback help move a design forward.
